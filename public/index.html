About AGMATIX AI PRO

AGMATIX AI PRO (Agricultural Matrix AI Professional) is an open-source, community-driven platform that harnesses the power of the Pi Network and artificial intelligence to transform global agricultural markets.

Our mission is to give small-scale farmers access to the same data, analytics, and marketplace tools that large agribusinesses enjoy — leveling the playing field and creating a fairer, more efficient agricultural economy.

  • ✅ 100% open-source — MIT licensed
  • ✅ Non-custodial Pi Network integration
  • ✅ AI models trained on global crop & weather data
  • ✅ Community governance via Pi consensus
